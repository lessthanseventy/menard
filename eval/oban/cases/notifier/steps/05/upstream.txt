Prevent newer execution from overwriting

Acking now verifies that the database hasn't been updated by another
execution. Writes from a new executor will no longer transition an acked
job, e.g. erroring or snoozing a job back out of `completed`.

This applies to ack operations in Basic, Dolphin, and Lite engines.
