Roll back attempt and count snoozes on snooze

Snoozing incremented `max_attempts` inflated retry timing and skewed
backoff with each snooze. Now snoozing matches Oban Pro and the
`attempt` is rolled back, so a snooze never consumes an attempt and
backoff stays accurate. Each snooze also increments a `snoozed` count in
the job's `meta`.
