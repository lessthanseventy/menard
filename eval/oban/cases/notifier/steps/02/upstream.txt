Support per-entry timezones in the crontab

Individual crontab entries may now override the plugin's timezone with a
`:timezone` option, e.g. `{"0 9 * * *", MyApp.Worker, timezone:
"America/Chicago"}`. Previously every entry was evaluated in a single
configured timezone, which forced a separate Cron instance for each
zone.
