Expose stager as a top-level service option

Previously staging could only be configured through the `stage_interval`
option, which is inconsistent with other services like cron, pruner, and
lifeline. The stager now accepts options directly, e.g. `stager:
[interval: 5000]`, `false` to disable it, or `{module, opts}` tuple for
alternative implementations.

The `stage_interval` option is soft-deprecated but still accepted, along
with the legacy `poll_interval` and older plugin style.
